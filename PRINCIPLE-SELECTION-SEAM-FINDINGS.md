# Principle Selection Seam — Investigation Findings

> **This document is a historical snapshot, not a living specification.**
> It records the result of the Principle Selection Seam investigation
> (read-only, no implementation authority) **as of 2026-08-12**, run to answer
> one question: what is the smallest semantically correct way for the
> structured coach to select one founder-authored teaching principle?
>
> **Do not edit this file to record new decisions.** The architectural defects
> named in Section 6 are recorded here deliberately and were **not** fixed
> during this investigation. If any of them is later fixed or superseded, the
> ADR or work order that does so is the source of truth — this file stays as
> it was.

---

## Method

Both coaches were run over all 15 cases of the `coaching-calibration` corpus
with **no LLM calls**. Teaching Move selection, readiness, opportunity
selection, and habit lookup are all deterministic, so every number below is
reproducible from the repository as it stood at commit `d255809`.

## Finding 1 — `TeachingMoveType` is not semantically sufficient to select a teaching principle

Three independent lines of evidence.

### 1a. The frozen architecture already declares the opposite dependency direction

`decision-engine/src/engine.ts:178-179`:

```ts
const habit = pick.related_habits[0]!;
const fork  = knowledgeCheckFork(habit, input.learner_model);
```

`knowledgeCheckFork` (`decision-engine/src/rules.ts:81`) takes **habit as
input** and returns the *intervention* (I1 supply / I3 elicit / I7 reframe).
In the frozen design the principle is **upstream** of the coaching action.
Habit is sourced from the **opportunity** in both places it appears —
`OPPORTUNITY_COPY[id].habit_id` and `REGISTRY[].related_habits` — and never
from an action. Deriving habit from Teaching Move would invert the founder's
own dependency direction.

### 1b. The corpus cross-tab is fully saturated

Deterministic-path habit against structured-path Teaching Move, all 15 cases:

| Structured move | Habits the frozen path chose |
|---|---|
| `highlight_strength` (10 cases) | **H4** ×7 (001, 002, 004, 007, founder, nursing, support) · **H2** ×3 (005, 008, checkout) |
| `request_number` (3 cases) | **H2** ×2 (006, thin) · **H4** ×1 (009) |
| `request_constraint` (2 cases) | **H4** (010) · **H2** (sales) |

- **A stable move→habit mapping exists?** No. Every move that fires produces
  both habits.
- **One move can lead to multiple habits?** Yes — all three firing moves do.
- **One habit can be reached through multiple moves?** Yes — H4 arrives via
  `highlight_strength` (001), `request_number` (009), and
  `request_constraint` (010).

`request_tool` and `contrast_attempts` never fire on this corpus:
`contrast_attempts` requires a retry graph, and `request_tool` requires
quantity **and** constraint **and** no action. Two of the five moves are
untested here.

### 1c. Range mismatch — the proof that does not depend on the frozen habit being correct

Even a *perfect* mapping reaches at most two of the eight principles.
`request_number ↔ H5` is the one honest correspondence.
`request_constraint` and `request_tool` both gesture at specificity and
collide on H4. `highlight_strength` is an affirmation and carries no lesson.
`contrast_attempts` describes a delta between two answers, not a principle.
**H3, H8, H9, H10 and H12 have no move that could ever express them.**

### 1d. The HF-001 contradiction, sharpened

Case 001 (= HF-001, `coaching-benchmark/src/manifest.ts:40`): the frozen path
selects `O5_vagueness → H4` — *"Name the machine, the week, the part"* — on a
12-node / 3-edge graph that `teachingMove.ts:82-88` explicitly names as the
answer that must **not** be downgraded. The structured path selects
`highlight_strength` + ADVANCE.

These are not two values of one variable. They are opposite verdicts on
whether anything is missing. Under a move→habit derivation, HF-001 receives
**no principle at all**, and the founder's own capstone answer carries zero
transferable teaching.

## Finding 2 — Six of the eight teaching principles are unreachable from the structured coach's current representation

The limit is not the move vocabulary; it is the *input*.

`GraphNode` (`evidence-runtime/src/evidenceGraph.ts:13`) is:

```ts
{ id, evidence_class, claim_type, quote, speaker_assertion, admitted_by }
```

over closed enums of ten claim types and five relationship types
(`evidence-validator/src/types.ts:24,45`).

| Principle | What it needs | Present in the graph? |
|---|---|---|
| H3 ownership | agency — who performed the action | **No** field carries it |
| H8 buried lede | position of a claim in the answer | **No** — `source_span` is dropped when the graph is built |
| H9 weak close | position of a claim in the answer | **No** — same |
| H10 filler | disfluency | **No** — filler never becomes a claim |
| H12 negativity | stance / sentiment toward people | **No** claim type carries it |
| H2 arc | problem → action → outcome ordering | partial (claim types + `temporal_sequence`) |
| H4 specificity | vague vs. concrete | **No** — declined deliberately, `teachingMove.ts:6-11` |
| H5 measurement | a quantity bound to a result | **Yes** — `quantity_binding` |

Six of the eight founder-authored principles are unreachable from the
structured path's entire input, not merely from its move.

## Finding 3 — H3 / ownership is effectively unreachable in the frozen path as well

`O4_ownership_hiding` fires as a **candidate** on 4 of the 15 cases — 005,
008, 009 and checkout — and carries the **highest confidence in its pool** on
005 and 008 (0.75 against 0.50). It is selected **zero** times.

Mechanism: `O4_ownership_hiding` declares `O3_missing_result` as an upstream
dependency, so the JA-01 root-cause rule (`decision-engine/src/rules.ts:42-48`)
demotes it as *"symptom this turn"* whenever both fire. On 009 it survives
that demotion but loses the tiebreak to `O5_vagueness`, which is itself
classified root-cause.

Across all 15 cases, only **2 of the 9 opportunities** (`O5_vagueness` ×9,
`O3_missing_result` ×6) and only **2 of the 8 habits** (H4, H2) are ever
selected.

The consequence for the SOD ownership anchor is not "structured lags
deterministic". **Neither path teaches ownership.**

## Finding 4 — Two independent habit mappings exist and are not mechanically enforced

| Mapping | Location | Consumed by |
|---|---|---|
| `REGISTRY[].related_habits` | `opportunity-engine/src/registry.ts:31` | the intervention fork (`decision-engine/src/engine.ts:178`) |
| `OPPORTUNITY_COPY[id].habit_id` | `conversation-engine/src/registry.ts:22` | the `WHY_IT_MATTERS` copy lookup (`generator.ts:151`) |

They agree today. Nothing checks that they continue to.

## Finding 5 — `O3_missing_result` has multiple related habits, and the frozen engine selects `[0]` by array position

`REGISTRY` declares `O3_missing_result: related_habits: ["H2", "H5"]`
(`opportunity-engine/src/registry.ts:33`). `decision-engine/src/engine.ts:178`
takes `pick.related_habits[0]!`.

H5 is therefore discarded by array position, not by coaching judgment. On this
corpus `O3_missing_result` is selected on 6 of 15 cases, so this is the
single most frequently exercised habit decision in the frozen path — and it is
made by an index.

## Finding 6 — The second exported coaching-runtime entry point does not receive readiness

`runNarratorLive` calls `buildUserMessage(act, evidence)` with no readiness
argument (`coaching-runtime/src/narrator.ts:120`). `runCoachingRuntime`
(`coaching-runtime/src/pipeline.ts:82`) goes through it, and therefore always
renders **ADVANCE** on `highlight_strength`, ignoring the readiness decoupling
committed in `d255809`.

Latent, not live — no in-repo caller uses `runCoachingRuntime` — but it is
exported from `coaching-runtime/src/index.ts:8`. Any future decision added
only to `runStructuredCoach` will fork the same way.

## Finding 7 — The deterministic representation does not contain enough semantic information to support several founder-authored principles

Every signal available to the structured coach today was inventoried:

| Signal | Carries a principle-level judgment? |
|---|---|
| `selectTeachingMove` | No — node/edge counts only, by explicit design (`teachingMove.ts:6-11`) |
| `computeResultReadiness` | No — one bit, self-documented as a structural proxy (`structuredCoach.ts:38-44`) |
| `grounding.ts` / `guardrail.ts` | No — post-hoc checks on coach output |
| `graph.non_admissible` (Class C) | No — hypotheses about the world, not about the answer's habits |
| raw transcript | Not available — `runStructuredCoach`'s only input is the graph (`structuredCoach.ts:51`) |

Reusing the frozen path's opportunity instead was tested and rejected on
evidence: that machinery emits only 2 of its 9 opportunities across the whole
corpus, so it reaches the same H4/H2 ceiling, and it judges the raw transcript
lexically, so its output is not attributable to validated evidence — the
structured path's core guarantee.

---

## Finding 8 — SOD is therefore warranted as an experiment, and its capability is not yet proven

The two things needed — perception of ownership, and judgment of whether a
result has *substance* rather than merely result-shaped structure — exist in
neither vocabulary, and both require reading quote text semantically. That is
what a Semantic Opportunity Detector would do.

Two honest qualifications:

1. SOD is necessary for the eight-principle set, **not** for H5 alone.
   `quantity_binding` already determines that one deterministically.
2. Nothing in this investigation demonstrates that an LLM *can* perceive these
   signals reliably. That is an open empirical question, not an established
   capability.

## Recommended seam (recommendation only — not authorized here)

Principle selection should live **alongside** Teaching Move selection, as its
own function over a shared semantic signal, called in `runStructuredCoach` at
the same layer as `computeResultReadiness`
(`coaching-runtime/src/structuredCoach.ts:89-93`):

```
selectPrinciple(graph, semantic?) → habit_id | null
```

- It matches the frozen architecture's own dependency direction (Finding 1a).
- Readiness decoupling is the precedent: a second deterministic decision that
  is not the move, computed beside it, rendered by the Narrator, never chosen
  by it.
- It degrades honestly. With today's inputs it returns `H5` or `null`, and
  `null` is the correct output — strictly better than a mechanically derived
  wrong principle.
- Teaching-principle sourcing is settled: leaf import of `WHY_IT_MATTERS`
  from `conversation-engine/src/registry`, no copies.

Rejected alternatives: **after** Teaching Move (inherits the axis mismatch);
**downstream of SOD only** (blocks the H5 case, which is honest today).
